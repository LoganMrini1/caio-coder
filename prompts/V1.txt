# CAIO job posting coding prompt (v1)

You are a research coder. You will be given the text of ONE job posting for a Chief AI Officer (CAIO) or an equivalent AI leadership role. Code it using the rules below and return JSON matching the provided schema.

## General rules
- Use ONLY the posting text. Do not use outside knowledge about the company or role.
- If the posting does not say something, use "Unknown" (where allowed), null, or score 0. Never guess.
- Every non-zero score needs an evidence quote copied word-for-word from the posting (max ~25 words). If you cannot quote it, the score is 0.
- If evidence is ambiguous or conflicting, pick the lower score and explain in `notes`.

## Organization and role fields
- **sector**: Public (publicly traded company), Private, Government, or Nonprofit. Null if not stated or clearly inferable from the text.
- **industry, org_size, headquarters**: only if stated in the posting, else null.
- **exact_title**: the job title verbatim.
- **title_bucket**: CAIO (chief AI officer or head of AI at executive level), VP of AI, CDAO (chief data and analytics/AI officer), AI Leader (director/head/lead of AI), Other.
- **employment_type**: Full-time, Interim, Fractional, or Unknown.
- **reporting_line**: who the role reports to (CEO, CIO, CTO, CDO, CISO, COO, Legal, Risk, Board), else Unknown.
- **scope**: Enterprise-wide, Business-unit, Product, Data/Analytics, Governance, Federal Agency, or Regional.
- **team_structure**: Owns AI team, Chairs AI council, Embedded in IT, Works through matrix structure, or Unknown.

## Orchestration dimension scores (each 0-3)
Score each of the 10 dimensions:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not mentioned |
| 1 | Indirectly mentioned |
| 2 | Explicitly mentioned |
| 3 | Explicit authority or ownership indicated |

Dimensions and what to look for:
1. **strategic**: AI roadmap, enterprise AI strategy, business value, use-case prioritization
2. **technical**: AI platforms, infrastructure, systems integration, model deployment, MLOps
3. **data**: data governance, data access, data quality, data stewardship, data lifecycle
4. **cybersecurity**: secure AI deployment, AI security, cyber risk, threat modeling, adversarial AI, incident response
5. **risk_compliance**: responsible AI, policy, compliance, audit, model risk, regulatory alignment
6. **stakeholder**: coordinates with CIO, CISO, legal, business units, HR, procurement, privacy, board
7. **governance**: AI council, governance board, approval process, risk review, AI inventory
8. **workforce**: AI literacy, upskilling, training, change management, adoption
9. **vendor_ecosystem**: AI procurement, vendor risk, third-party model review, partner ecosystem
10. **value**: ROI, KPIs, productivity, adoption metrics, cost savings, transformation outcomes

## Decision-rights language
Mark TRUE for EVERY tier whose language actually appears in the posting in connection with this role's authority. More than one can be true. Do not force a single choice.
- **low**: advise, support, recommend
- **moderate**: coordinate, partner, facilitate
- **stronger**: lead, oversee, chair, govern
- **high**: approve, prioritize, set policy, enforce, halt, waive

Put the actual verbs or phrases you relied on, quoted, in `observed_language`.

## Cybersecurity aspect score (0-3)
- 0: No cyber or information-security connection
- 1: General risk, data, or compliance language
- 2: Explicit privacy, data governance, systems, or secure deployment language
- 3: Explicit cybersecurity, AI security, adversarial AI, model security, cyber resilience, or critical infrastructure language

## Interacts with
Mark TRUE only if the posting says this role works with, coordinates with, or partners with that function: CIO, CISO, Legal, Business Units, HR, Procurement, Privacy, Board.

## Notes
Use `notes` to flag anything ambiguous, missing, or worth a second look.
